How I work

Four opinions I'll defend

Trust before features

Nobody adopts an insight they can't source. Lineage, definitions and owners are product work, not a data-team chore, and they buy you the right to ship the interesting things.

I read the query plan

Most "UX problems" in analytics are a slow join wearing a costume. Being able to sit with the engineer in the semantic layer changes what the roadmap can promise.

A test, or an opinion

Every bet gets a hypothesis, a metric and a stopping rule written down before launch. Half of mine have been wrong; that's the return on the process, not a failure of it.

Consulting is scoping

As a consultant you get one shot at the real problem. I spend the first two weeks on the question and its owner, then move fast, because by then the fast direction is the right one.

The person behind it

Electronics engineer, accidental analyst, deliberate PM

I started in nano-electronics at NIT Kurukshetra, fell into SQL at HCL, and found that I liked the argument about the number more than the number. Seven years later I've done the analyst's job, the platform PM's job and the consultant's job on the same class of problem, which is why I can tell in a week whether a data product has a tooling problem or a trust problem. Based in Gurugram, working with a UK team.
